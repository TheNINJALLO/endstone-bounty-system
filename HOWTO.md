# How to Use the Bounty System In-Game

This guide explains how to use all features of the Bounty System plugin using the **PvP Altar** item.

---

## The PvP Altar Item

The **PvP Altar** is your gateway to the entire bounty system.

### How to Use:
1. Hold the PvP Altar item in your hand
2. **Right-click** (use/interact) with the item
3. A menu will appear showing your current status and all options

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Opt Into PvP
Before you can participate in any PvP or bounty activities, you need to enable PvP:

1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"Toggle PvP"**
3. Click **"Enable PvP"** to opt in
4. **Your first PvP opt-in is FREE** with no cooldown!

### Step 2: Opt Into Bounties (Optional)
If you want to place bounties, claim bounties, or have bounties placed on you:

1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"Toggle Bounty"** (only appears if PvP is enabled)
3. Click **"Enable Bounties"** to opt in
4. **Your first Bounty opt-in is FREE** with no cooldown!

---

## PvP Altar Menu Options

When you use the PvP Altar, you'll see your current status at the top:
- Your PvP status (Enabled/Disabled)
- Your Bounty status (Enabled/Disabled)
- Any active protections

### Available Buttons:

| Button | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Place Bounty** | Open the bounty placement form (requires Bounty enabled) |
| **Waive Protection/Cooldown** | Pay to remove protections or skip cooldowns |
| **Toggle PvP** | Enable or disable PvP participation |
| **Toggle Bounty** | Enable or disable Bounty participation (requires PvP) |
| **View Leaderboard** | See all active bounties on the server |

---

## Placing a Bounty

### Requirements:
- ✅ PvP must be enabled
- ✅ Bounties must be enabled
- ✅ You need enough money

### Steps:
1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"Place Bounty"**
3. Select the target player from the dropdown
   - Only players with Bounties enabled will appear
4. Enter the bounty amount (minimum is usually 100)
5. Click **Submit** to confirm
6. The money is deducted from your balance and the target is notified

---

## Viewing Active Bounties

To see all current bounties on the server:

1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"View Leaderboard"**
3. A list will appear showing:
   - All players with bounties
   - Total bounty amounts
   - Who contributed to each bounty

---

## Claiming a Bounty

Bounties are claimed **automatically** when you kill a player who has a bounty.

### Requirements:
- ✅ Both you and the target have Bounties enabled
- ✅ Neither of you is in a "No PvP" safe zone
- ✅ You are NOT in a "PvP Zone" (these block bounty claims)
- ✅ The target is not under protection

### What Happens:
1. Kill the player with a bounty
2. The full bounty amount is added to your money
3. A server-wide message announces the claim
4. The victim gets temporary protection

---

## Managing Your Status

### Check Your Status
Use the PvP Altar - your status is displayed at the top of the menu.

### Opting Out
1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"Toggle Bounty"** → Disable (to stop bounty participation)
3. Select **"Toggle PvP"** → Disable (to stop all PvP)
   - ⚠️ Disabling PvP also automatically disables Bounties

### Cooldowns
After your first opt-in, there are cooldowns:
- **Opt-in cooldown**: Usually 1 day
- **Opt-out cooldown**: Usually 3 days

---

## Waiving Protections & Cooldowns

If you want to bypass protections or cooldowns early:

1. Use the PvP Altar item
2. Select **"Waive Protection/Cooldown"**
3. Choose what to waive:
   - **New Player Protection** - Remove your newbie shield
   - **Post-Death Protection** - Remove protection after being killed
   - **PvP Opt-In Cooldown** - Enable PvP immediately
   - **PvP Opt-Out Cooldown** - Disable PvP immediately
   - **Bounty Opt-In Cooldown** - Enable Bounties immediately
   - **Bounty Opt-Out Cooldown** - Disable Bounties immediately
4. Pay the cost to waive

---

## Understanding Safe Zones

### No PvP Zones (Complete Safety)
- You **cannot** attack or be attacked
- Often used for spawn areas and shops
- Shows a message when you enter/exit

### PvP Zones (Arena Style)
- **Everyone can fight** here, even if PvP is disabled
- All protections are bypassed
- **Bounties CANNOT be claimed** in these zones
- Perfect for practice fighting without losing bounties

---

## Tips for New Players

1. **Don't rush to opt in** - Use your new player protection to get established
2. **Start with PvP only** - You can fight without bounty participation
3. **Use PvP Zones to practice** - Fight without risking bounties
4. **Check the leaderboard** - Know who has bounties before hunting
5. **Save money for waivers** - In case you need to opt out quickly
